Privacy centre
KaivoFlow has no KaivoFlow account and no KaivoFlow servers. These statements are checkable in the source.
Stays on this phone
  • Flows, Step settings, variables and Run history — a local database.
  • Frozen (pinned) Step outputs.
  • Saved files written by Device Steps.
Leaves only when a Step asks it to
  • An HTTP, AI or app Step sends that Step's data to the provider you configured, with your own key.
  • No analytics SDK. No crash reporter.
Never read
  • When notification access is granted, notification contents are received for matching and may be used by configured Flows.
  • When SMS access is granted, matching message data may be used by configured Flows.
  • Credentials are Keystore-encrypted and excluded from backup.
